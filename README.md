# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project features an interactive sales performance dashboard designed to analyze retail transaction data, track key performance indicators (KPIs), and uncover operational insights across different regions, product categories, and customer segments. 

The dashboard provides a macro-to-micro view of the business, allowing stakeholders to filter data dynamically by **Region** and **Year** (spanning 2014–2017) to make data-driven decisions.

## Key Features & Insights
* **Executive KPI Summary:** Tracks critical business metrics at a glance, including Total Sales ($5.82M), Average Sales Value ($582.37), Total Transactions (9,994), and Total Quantity Sold (25,123).
* **Logistics & Shipping Optimization:** Analyzes sales distribution by shipping mode, revealing that **Standard Class** drives the vast majority of volume (58%).
* **Geographic Breakdown:** Visualizes regional transaction volumes (led by the East and West regions) and highlights top-performing revenue cities, with **New York City** and **Los Angeles** leading major markets.
* **Seasonality & Demand Forecasting:** Features a monthly trend line of ordered quantities, capturing key demand surges—particularly a massive peak in **November** (3,039 units).
* **Inventory & Product Analysis:** Segments transaction volume by product category, highlighting **Office Supplies** as the highest-frequency transaction driver (6,026 transactions).
* **Customer Segmentation:** Breaks down order quantities by customer type, showing that the **Consumer** segment is the primary engine of volume (13,030 units).

## How to Use the Dashboard
1. Open the source file (`.pbix` or `.xlsx`) included in this repository.
2. Use the left-hand slicer panel to filter by specific **Regions** (Central, East, South, West).
3. Drill down into specific operational years (**2014 - 2017**) to observe year-over-year performance trends.
